InputOTP takes Dark Factory's field on the web, iOS and Android alike, since no platform ships a one-time-code control. Each cell is now its own field, set apart from the next, where the web drew one connected run: the translucent well at a 10px corner, as tall as an Input of the same size (34, 40 and 46px squares at small, base and large; 6, 8 and 10px apart), whose hairline turns violet on the cell the next character lands in, with no halo. The digits are semibold in the foreground ink, and the caret is a violet bar on every platform. The caret now blinks on the web too, on the same one-second cycle the iOS and Android fields had (visible, a short fade, hidden, a short fade back), and on every platform it runs on the kit's loop primitive: the native driver on iOS and Android and a compositor CSS animation on the web, so the blink costs no React render per frame; Reduce Motion holds it solid. On an iPhone and on Android a cell grows taller where it is shorter than the platform's 44pt or 48dp touch minimum, so the field keeps its touch area. A disabled field no longer fades: each cell keeps a hairline frame with no fill and muted digits, and paints no glass. Under glass each cell is the clear well the other web fields are.
