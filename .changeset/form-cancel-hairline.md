Form's cancel button is the outline button on every platform. On the web that is Dark Factory's hairline pill: Dark Factory's form calls its cancel a `ghost` button, and its ghost button is the neutral hairline look that Canvas's `outline` Button draws, while Canvas's `ghost` has no border. On Android it stays the Material 3 outlined button. The Form page also says that a two-column form's odd last field takes the whole line, which is how one field gets a full-width row.
