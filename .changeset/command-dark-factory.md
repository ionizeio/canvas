Command takes Dark Factory's palette on the web, iOS and Android alike, since no platform ships a command palette. The palette is Dark Factory's menu panel at its dialog's 18px corner with an 8px inset; the search row is its search field, a 15px search glyph beside a 13px semibold query, over a rule that turns violet and thickens while the search holds focus; the results are its menu rows (33px, 2px apart, 12.5px bold labels, 14px icons) under uppercase eyebrow headings, with the active row on the menu's pressed fill; and the footer is a hairline note of key hints in muted 11px type. The palette sits 8px below its trigger. The collapsed trigger is now the Input's field, a translucent well at a 10px corner whose hairline turns violet while the palette is open, holding the search glyph and a tracked uppercase placeholder before its shortcut; under glass it is the clear well the other web fields are, where it used to be unfilled. Under glass the active and pressed rows are ink tints on the palette rather than opaque fills. On an iPhone and on Android the rows, the search row and the trigger grow to the platform's 44pt or 48dp touch minimum, and Android's rows keep their ripple.
